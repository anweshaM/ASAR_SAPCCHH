The data and information about the methodology and analysis of the study "Critical Assessment of National and Sub-National Climate Change and Health Action Plans of India" by the Association for Socially Applicable Research (ASAR) are provided in this repository.

This repository contains the action plan documents that were analysed in the study. The State Action Plans for Climate Change and Human Health (SAPCCHH) documents of the Indian states and union territories are in the sapcchh_2023 folder. These documents were downloaded from the NCDC website (https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/) in 2023, after which some of the documents might have undergone changes.

The spreadsheets contain information on:
(1) Analysis_sapcchh_statewise_methodology.xlsx: the methodology protocol used to assess the SAPCCHH documents, 
(2) Analysis_sapcchh_statewise_evidence.xlsx: the detailed excerpts and evidence used to decide the scores, and 
(3) Analysis_sapcchh_statewise_scores.xlsx: the secondary criteria and the final average scores.

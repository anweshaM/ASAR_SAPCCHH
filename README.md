The data and information pertaining to the methodology and analysis of the study "Critical Assessment of National and Sub-National Climate Change and Health Action Plans of India" by the Association for Socially Applicable Research (ASAR) is provided in this repository.

This repository contains the action plan documents that were analysed in the study. The State Action Plans for Climate Change and Human Health (SAPCCHH) documents of the Indian states and union territories in the sapcchh_2023 folder. These documents were downloaded from the NCDC website (https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/) in 2023, after which some of the documents might have undergone changes.

The spreadsheet Analysis_sapcchh_statewise_methodology_evidence_scores.xlsx contains 3 sheets with information on:
(1) the methodology protocol used to assess the SAPCCHH documents (Methodology_Protocol), 
(2) the detailed excerpts and evidence used to decide the scores (All_States_Evidence), and 
(3) the final scores (Scores).
